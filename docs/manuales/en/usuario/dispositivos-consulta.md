## Devices

`DEV` menu. It is the inventory of your organization's network devices. Use the search (identifier, name or vendor)
and the criticality and status filters; when you choose a device, its details appear on the right: type, vendor,
site, criticality and status.

![Device inventory with the details of the chosen device](dispositivos.png)

Your profile can view the inventory. Registering, editing or deactivating devices is done by your organization's
administrator and the system administrator. An **inactive** device keeps its history, but does not accept new
configurations or audits.

The device **criticality** (low, medium, high or critical) weighs on the risk level and decides which findings
generate alerts on the dashboards.
