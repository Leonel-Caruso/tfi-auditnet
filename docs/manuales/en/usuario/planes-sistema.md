## Plans and requests

`PLN` menu. Organizations request plan changes from *My plan*; you resolve them here. You also maintain the catalog
reference prices.

![Requests to resolve and plan catalog](planes.png)

### Resolve a request

In **Requests to resolve** each request shows the organization, the current and requested plan, the billing (monthly
or yearly), who requested it and their comment.

- **Approve:** you can write a reply to the client. The plan applies immediately; if the organization uses more than
  the new plan allows, the approval is rejected.
- **Reject:** the reason is required and the client sees it in My plan.

In both cases the person who requested the change receives an email. Resolved requests remain in **History**.

### Catalog and prices

| Plan | Limits |
|---|---|
| Piloto | Up to 10 devices and 5 users, trial of 60 to 90 days |
| Inicial | Up to 50 devices and 15 users |
| Profesional | Up to 150 devices and 40 users |
| Empresa | Custom limits and price |

**Edit price** changes the monthly reference price (empty = *on request* or *free of charge*) and the terms. Limits
cannot be edited. The platform does not charge: prices are for reference and the change is recorded in the
transaction log.

> To assign a plan without a prior request, use **Change plan** in *Organizations and sites*.
