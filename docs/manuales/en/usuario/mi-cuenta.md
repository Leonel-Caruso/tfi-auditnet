## My account

Open it from the user menu → **My account**.

![My account: your details and the password change](cuenta.png)

### Your details

The **Identity** panel shows your username, email, organization, profile and when the session expires. To change
your name, email, organization or profile, ask an administrator.

### Change your password

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Enter the **new password** and repeat it. The conditions are ticked as you type.
3. Click **Change password**.

Changing it closes your other open sessions; the current one stays open.

### Language, theme and email notices

![Language, theme and email notices](cuenta-preferencias.png)

- **Language:** applies to the console, the guides and the emails AuditNet sends you.
- **Theme:** Light or Dark. It is saved in your account.
- **Email notices:** tick the ones you want to receive and click **Save notices**. Only the ones that apply to your
  profile are shown (see the *Email notices* chapter).
