## Users of your organization

`USR` menu. It lists the users of your organization. You manage those with **operational profiles**: network analyst,
technical auditor and IT management lead. Administrators are managed by the system administrator (they appear with
the label *managed by the system administrator*).

### Register a user

![New user of the organization](usuario-alta.png)

1. Click **+ user**.
2. Fill in **full name**, **email**, **username** (letters without accents, numbers, dot, hyphen or underscore; it
   cannot be changed later) and an **initial password** that meets the policy. The organization is yours.
3. Check one or more **profiles**.
4. Click **Register user**.

The user receives a welcome email with their username (without the password): share the initial password through a
secure channel. The new user counts toward the active user limit of your plan.

### Actions on a user

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Changes name, email and profiles. If you change the profile, their open sessions are closed. |
| **Password** | Assigns a new password, removes any lock and closes their sessions. |
| **Unlock** | Appears when the account was locked after 5 failed attempts. |
| **Disable / Enable** | Logical deletion: they cannot sign in and their sessions are closed, but their history is kept. |

The **Permission matrix** shows what each profile can do.
