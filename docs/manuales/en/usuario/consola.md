## The console

![Dashboard after signing in: side menu, top bar and access to the guide](tablero.png)

### Side menu

On the left is the menu with the screens your profile can use. Each icon has a short code; hover over it to see the
full name.

| Code | Screen | Code | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| `~/` | Dashboard | `BL` | Baselines |
| `CLI` | Client status | `RUL` | Rules |
| `ORG` | Organizations and sites | `RUN` | Audits |
| `USR` | Users | `HIS` | Audit history |
| `PLN` | My plan / Plans and requests | `LOG` | Findings |
| `BIT` | Logs | `ALR` | Critical alerts |
| `ADM` | Administration panel | `REP` | Reports |
| `DEV` | Devices | | |

Only the screens of your profile are shown.

### Top bar

- **Location:** on the left, where you are (for example `AuditNet / findings`).
- **Go to module:** in the center, type the name of a screen and press Enter to go straight there. For example
  `findings`, `alerts`, `reports` or `account`.
- **Connection status:** `online` means the services are responding.
- **User menu:** on the right, with your username.

### User menu

![User menu: language, theme, My account, guide and sign out](menu-usuario.png)

Here you change the **language** (ES, EN, PT) and the **theme** (Light or Dark); both are saved in your account and
used in any browser. You also have **My account**, the PDF **guide** for each profile you have and **Sign out**.

### User guide

At the top of the dashboard there is a **User guide** card with a download button for each profile of your account.
The PDF is downloaded in the console language. **Hide** removes the card in that browser; the guide is still available
in the user menu.

### Session

The session lasts a limited time (My account shows when it expires) and it also ends if an administrator changes your
profile, your organization or your password. In that case AuditNet takes you to the sign-in screen with a notice.
