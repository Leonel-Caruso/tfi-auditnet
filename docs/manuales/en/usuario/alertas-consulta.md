## Critical alerts

`ALR` menu. Each finding of **high or critical** severity generates an alert (at most one current alert per device and
rule). Filter by status, severity, device criticality, device and site.

![Prioritized alerts with the chosen alert](alertas.png)

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| active | nobody has taken it yet |
| in progress | it was assigned to a technical profile (network analyst or technical auditor) |
| closed | its finding was resolved or the risk was accepted |

When you choose an alert you see the finding that triggered it and who it was assigned to. Your profile can view it;
assigning or returning it is done by the network analyst and the IT management lead.
