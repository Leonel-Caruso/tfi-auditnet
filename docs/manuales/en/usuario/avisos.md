## Email notices

AuditNet sends emails in each recipient's language.

**Always (they cannot be turned off):**

- **New user:** when your account is created, with your username.
- **Password recovery:** the link you requested.
- **Plans:** to the system administrator when an organization requests a plan change, and to the requester when it is
  resolved.

**Audit notices (chosen in My account → Email notices):**

| Notice | What it includes | Received by |
|---|---|---|
| New critical alerts | one email per audit with its critical or high severity alerts | network analyst, IT management lead, organization administrator |
| Deadlines and outdated configurations | daily review: findings past their deadline and devices without a new configuration | network analyst, IT management lead, organization administrator |
| Monthly summary | in the first days of each month, the organization's activity for the previous month | IT management lead, organization administrator |

They are turned on by default. The technical auditor and the system administrator do not receive audit notices.
