## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Incorrect username or password* | Check upper and lower case and that Caps Lock is off. If you do not remember it, use **Forgot your password?** |
| *The account is locked* | After 5 failed attempts it is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, recover the password or ask an administrator to unlock it. |
| *Your session ended* | It expired or an administrator changed your profile, your organization or your password. Sign in again. |
| I cannot see a screen or a button | Your profile does not have that permission. The permission matrix is in *Users* (for administrators) and the first chapter of this guide summarizes what your profile does. |
| *There is no active baseline for the selected device type* | The organization has no active baseline for that device type. The technical auditor creates or activates it. |
| I cannot add devices or users | The plan limit was reached or the Piloto plan trial expired. The organization administrator requests a change in *My plan*. |
| Emails do not arrive | Check *My account → Email notices* and the spam folder. Audit notices depend on the profile. |
| The screen shows another language or theme | Change them in the user menu; they are saved in your account. |
| The guide does not download | Try from the user menu → *Guide*. If it still fails, tell the system administrator. |
