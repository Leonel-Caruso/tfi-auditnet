## My plan

`PLN` menu. It shows your organization's plan, how much of it is used, the available plans and the request history.

![Current plan, usage, pending request and catalog](mi-plan.png)

### Current plan and usage

The bars show the **active devices** and **active users** against the plan maximum. When the limit is reached, no
more can be added until the plan changes.

If your organization is on **Piloto**, the trial expiration date is shown. When it expires, the organization goes
into **read-only mode**: everything can be viewed and findings and alerts can still be handled, but devices or users
cannot be added, configurations cannot be imported and audits cannot be run until a plan is approved.

### Request a plan change

1. Click **Request change**.
2. Choose the **plan**, the **billing** (monthly or yearly) and, optionally, a **comment** (for example, how many
   sites you are adding).
3. Click **Send request**.

The system administrator receives an email notice and reviews it; you receive the reply by email. Meanwhile the
current plan stays in force and no other plan can be requested. **Cancel request** withdraws it.

Catalog prices are monthly reference prices: the sales team confirms the terms when approving.
