## Signing in to AuditNet

### Sign in

1. Open the AuditNet address your organization gave you in an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
2. Enter your **username or email** and your **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

![Sign-in screen with the theme and language selectors at the top right](acceso.png)

At the top right you can choose, before signing in, the **theme** (Light or Dark) and the **language** (ES, EN, PT).
If your account already has a saved theme and language, those are used once you sign in.

> **Lockout:** after 5 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an administrator to
> unlock it.

### Recover your password

1. On the sign-in screen click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your username or email and click **Send link**.
3. Check your email: you receive a link that is valid for **30 minutes** and can be used only once.
4. Open it, choose the new password, repeat it and click **Save password**.

![Requesting the link to create a new password](recuperar.png)

The email arrives in the language of the screen you requested it from. Saving the new password closes any open
sessions and removes the lockout caused by failed attempts.

The password must be **10 to 72 characters long**, **combine letters and numbers** and **not contain your
username**. The screen ticks each condition as you type.
