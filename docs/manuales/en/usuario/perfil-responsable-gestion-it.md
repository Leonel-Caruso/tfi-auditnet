## Your profile: IT management lead

AuditNet is a web platform for auditing the configuration of network devices (Cisco IOS/IOS-XE routers and
switches). Each organization registers its devices, defines how each device type must be configured (baselines and
rules), imports the actual configurations and audits them. The result is findings with evidence, alerts for the most
serious ones and reports for management.

As **IT management lead** you follow the status of your organization and set priorities: you do not need to operate
the devices.

| You can | Where |
|---|---|
| See the risk level, the indicators and the weekly trend | Dashboard, Client status |
| Assign critical alerts to a technical profile or return them | Critical alerts |
| View findings, history, devices and rules | Each screen, read-only |
| View and download reports as PDF or CSV | Reports |
| Check the plan and the change requests | My plan |
| Receive alerts, deadlines and the monthly summary by email | My account |
