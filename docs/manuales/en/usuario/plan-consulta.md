## My plan

`PLN` menu. It shows your organization's plan, how many active devices and users it uses against the maximum, the
plan catalog and the change requests (pending and previous, with the administrator's reply).

![Current plan, usage, pending request and catalog](mi-plan.png)

Plan changes are requested by your organization's administrator. If the plan is **Piloto** and the trial expires, the
organization goes into **read-only mode**: everything can be viewed and findings and alerts can be handled, but
devices or users cannot be added, configurations cannot be imported and audits cannot be run until a plan is approved.
