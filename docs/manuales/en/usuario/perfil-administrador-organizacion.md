## Your profile: organization administrator

AuditNet is a web platform for auditing the configuration of network devices (Cisco IOS/IOS-XE routers and
switches). Each organization registers its devices, defines how each device type must be configured (baselines and
rules), imports the actual configurations and audits them. The result is findings with evidence, alerts for the most
serious ones and reports for management.

As **organization administrator** you manage everything in **your organization**: its people, its sites, its
inventory and its plan. You do not see data from other organizations.

| You can | Where |
|---|---|
| Register, modify and disable your organization's sites | Organizations and sites |
| Set the review deadlines (when a finding becomes overdue and how often devices are re-audited) | Organizations and sites |
| Create users with operation profiles (network analyst, technical auditor, IT management lead), modify them, disable them, reset their password and unlock them | Users |
| Check the plan and request a change | My plan |
| Register, modify and deactivate devices | Devices |
| View baselines, rules, audits, findings, alerts and reports | Each screen, read-only |

> **Note:** importing configurations, running audits or changing the status of a finding are tasks of the operation
> profiles. If you also need to do them, ask the system administrator to add that profile to your account: a user can
> have several.
