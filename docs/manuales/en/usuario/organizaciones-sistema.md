## Organizations and sites

`ORG` menu. Client organizations are on the left; when you choose one, its sites appear on the right and its plan and
review deadlines below.

![Registered organizations and sites of the chosen organization](organizaciones.png)

### Register an organization

1. Click **+ organization**.
2. Fill in the **identifier** (short code, saved in uppercase) and the **legal name**. Both are unique on the
   platform.
3. Choose the **initial plan**:
   - **Piloto** (pilot): enter the trial days (60 to 90).
   - **Empresa** (enterprise): enter the agreed devices and users.
   - **Inicial** and **Profesional** use the catalog limits.
4. Click **Register organization**.

![New organization with its initial plan](organizacion-alta.png)

### Edit, disable or enable

With the organization selected, **Edit** changes the identifier or the legal name. **Disable** prevents its users
from signing in (open sessions are closed immediately); its sites, devices, audits and reports are kept. **Enable**
activates it again.

### Sites

- **+ site:** name (unique within the organization) and location.
- **Edit** changes that data.
- **Disable** on a site prevents assigning new devices to it; the devices it already has keep their history.

### Change the plan

In the **Plan and usage** panel click **Change plan**, choose the new plan and click **Apply plan**. It applies
immediately. If the organization uses more devices or users than the new plan allows, the change is rejected. If there
is a pending request, the panel shows it with a link to resolve it.

### Review deadlines

Each organization defines within how many days a finding must be fixed according to its severity, how often a device
is audited again and after how many days its configuration counts as outdated. It is explained in the chapter about
your organization and works the same for any organization you choose.
