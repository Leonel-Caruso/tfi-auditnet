## Audit reports

`REP` menu. A report gathers audits with their executive summary, findings, severity, impact and evidence. It is saved
as a **snapshot**: it reflects the status of the findings at the time it was issued. Your profile can view and
download them; the network analyst and the technical auditor generate them.

![Document view of the report](reporte-documento.png)

When you choose a report from the **Generated reports** list, the document is shown: general data, executive summary
with compliance, scope, distribution of findings, included audits, details of each finding and notes.

- **Download PDF:** prints the document on A4 with page numbers, in the console language.
- **Download CSV:** for Excel, one row per finding. In Spanish and Portuguese it uses `;` as separator and in English
  `,`.
